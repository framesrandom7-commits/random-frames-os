# Random Frames – Founder & Co-Founder Roles and Responsibilities

---

# Founder

## Position
Founder & Creative Director

## Mission
Lead the creative vision of Random Frames by producing exceptional visual content, maintaining creative excellence, and driving the long-term growth and identity of the brand.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Creative Leadership
- Define and maintain the creative vision of Random Frames.
- Ensure brand consistency across all creative work.
- Set and maintain photography and videography quality standards.
- Approve all final creative deliverables.

### Production
- Plan and execute all photography and videography projects.
- Lead all commercial shoots and productions.
- Create shot lists, mood boards, and production plans.
- Manage camera equipment, lighting, and production gear.
- Ensure every project meets the company's creative standards.

### Brand Development
- Develop the Random Frames portfolio.
- Build and strengthen the company's visual identity.
- Research industry trends and introduce creative improvements.
- Maintain the overall quality and reputation of the brand.

### Networking & Business Development
- Build relationships with potential clients and industry professionals.
- Attend networking opportunities and industry events.
- Introduce Random Frames to prospective clients.
- Identify new business opportunities.

*Note: Once a lead is generated, ownership of client communication, follow-up, and sales is transferred to the Co-Founder.*

### Equipment & Technology
- Manage all creative equipment.
- Recommend equipment purchases.
- Maintain production tools and software.
- Improve creative workflows.

### Leadership
- Guide the creative direction of the company.
- Maintain high creative standards.
- Mentor future creative team members.

## Founder Owns

- Creative Direction
- Photography
- Videography
- Production
- Portfolio
- Brand Identity
- Creative Quality
- Equipment Management
- Networking

## Founder KPIs

- Creative quality
- Client satisfaction
- Portfolio growth
- Production efficiency
- Brand consistency
- Repeat clients

# Co-Founder

## Position
Co-Founder & Head of Operations

## Mission
Manage the operational side of Random Frames by delivering an outstanding client experience, maintaining efficient business operations, and supporting sustainable company growth.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Client Management
- Manage all client communication.
- Respond to enquiries.
- Conduct follow-ups.
- Schedule meetings.
- Understand client requirements.
- Build long-term client relationships.
- Collect client feedback and testimonials.

### Sales & Lead Management
- Own the complete sales pipeline.
- Record and qualify all leads.
- Schedule discovery calls.
- Prepare quotations and proposals.
- Conduct negotiations where appropriate.
- Convert leads into confirmed projects.
- Maintain the CRM and lead database.

### Project Management
- Schedule projects and shoots.
- Coordinate timelines.
- Monitor project progress.
- Manage deadlines.
- Coordinate revisions.
- Ensure timely project delivery.

### Operations
- Develop and improve Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- Maintain company documentation.
- Organize internal workflows.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Standardize business processes.

### Social Media Management
- Plan and manage content calendars.
- Schedule social media posts.
- Write captions.
- Manage community engagement.
- Track performance and analytics.
- Grow the online presence of Random Frames.

### Finance Coordination
- Generate quotations.
- Generate invoices.
- Track payments.
- Follow up on outstanding invoices.
- Maintain business expense records.
- Coordinate financial documentation.

### Administration
- Manage calendars and meetings.
- Organize business records.
- Maintain client database.
- Coordinate with vendors and external partners.
- Support day-to-day business administration.

## Co-Founder Owns

- Client Success
- Client Communication
- Lead Management
- Sales Pipeline
- CRM
- Operations
- Project Management
- Social Media
- Administration
- Delivery Coordination
- Payment Follow-ups

## Co-Founder KPIs

- Lead response time
- Lead conversion rate
- Client satisfaction
- On-time project delivery
- CRM accuracy
- Operational efficiency
- Social media consistency
- Payment collection rate
- Repeat clients

# Joint Responsibilities

Both the Founder and Co-Founder are jointly responsible for:

- Building the Random Frames brand.
- Long-term business growth.
- Strategic planning.
- Pricing strategy.
- Company registration.
- Equity discussions.
- Financial planning.
- Hiring future team members.
- Major equipment purchases.
- Strategic partnerships.
- Business expansion.
- Company policies.
- Annual business planning.
- Protecting the reputation and values of Random Frames.
